## What is wristkit?

wristkit is an open-source CLI + component library that lets you ship Apple Health visualizations on a personal site, portfolio, or athlete-facing web app — without writing a backend, without a third-party SaaS, and without storing data outside infrastructure you own.

Run `npx wristkit init`, point it at your own Supabase, import an iOS Shortcut on your iPhone, and minutes later your Apple Watch data is rendering in your Next.js app.

**Zero telemetry. MIT-licensed. Single-user in v1.**

## Who is it for?

- Developers who want their own health dashboard on their personal site
- Athletes who want to share training data without giving it to a third party
- Anyone who wants to own their health data end-to-end

## How it works

```
iOS Shortcut → POST /api/healthkit → Supabase → React components
```

1. The iOS Shortcut reads your Apple Health data and posts it to your Next.js app
2. A route handler validates and writes it to your Supabase Postgres database
3. React components query your database and render the data

wristkit never touches your data. We ship code — you run it on your infrastructure.

## What ships in v1

- `TodayActivityCard` — Move, Exercise, and Steps rings with five states
- `wristkit init` — scaffolds your project
- `wristkit add` — installs components from the registry
- `wristkit update` — shows diffs before overwriting
- `wristkit shortcut` — prints iOS Shortcut setup instructions
- A hosted registry at `wristkit.vercel.app`

## Stack requirements

- Next.js 15 (App Router)
- A Supabase project (free tier works)
- An iPhone with the Shortcuts app
